Energy Use of Static Analysis Tools in Developer Laptops

Abstract
This synthetic sample paper was written for MetaSense pipeline validation; it is not a real publication. It deliberately describes its method without a methodology heading.

Introduction
Static analysis tools run continuously in modern editors. The goal of this study is to measure how much energy they consume on developer laptops.

What We Did
We selected 12 open-source Python repositories and ran three static analysis tools on each one. Energy was measured with a hardware power meter attached to a laptop with a 4-core CPU and 16 GB of RAM. Each run was repeated 5 times and we report the mean and standard deviation. The metrics were energy in joules and wall-clock latency.

Findings
The most expensive tool used roughly four times more energy than the cheapest one, compared with a baseline of the editor running without any analysis.

Conclusion
Tool choice matters for battery life. A limitation is that only one laptop model was measured, which limits generalizability.
